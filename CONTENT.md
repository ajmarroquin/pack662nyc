## Pack content

Text, imagery, and branding belonging to Cub Scout Pack 662 are not licensed for
reuse. This includes written page content, any photographs, and anything
identifying the pack.

If you are building a site for your own pack, the structure of this repository
is the useful part and you are welcome to it. Take the templates and the schemas;
write your own words.

The hero scene (`src/components/HeroScene.astro`) and the section icons

They exist instead of photography on purpose. See below.

## Photographs

**No youth photographs are in this repository, and none will be added.**

Committing an image to a public repository publishes it permanently. Commit
history survives deleting the file, and deletion does not reach forks, clones,
or caches. Photographs of Scouts, if the pack ever wants them online, belong
behind the member tool at its own subdomain, never here.

The same applies to youth last names, in page content and in future photo
captions alike.
