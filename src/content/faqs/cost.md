question: "What does it cost?"
answer: "$145 to register your child with Scouting America and the council each year, which renews on the date you joined. We have our own pack dues, $120 a year, which run from September and are invoiced through Scoutbook once you register. This covers all of the materials we use to complete adventures as well as your pinewood derby car and rainguter regatta boat! Uniform and handbook are separate, we aim to reduce the cost impact of uniforms by hosting a uniform exchange. As kids grow out of uniforms, you can donate to it, and hopefully find a larger uniform from someone else. Campouts and some optional events have their own costs."
