question: "Do Cub Scouts camp?"
answer: "Yes. The whole pack camps twice a year at our council Cub Scout Activity days. Our Webelos and Arrow of Light dens camp twice more on top of that, which may include a summer camp and a joint campout with the Scouts BSA troop. Cub scout camping is family camping; at this age nobody is dropped off."
