question: "How does the pack keep kids safe?"
answer: "Every leader completes Scouting America's Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT) each year and passes a background check before taking a role. No adult is ever one-on-one with a child who is not their own. We ask every Akela to take the training too, free and online, so the whole pack is using the same language and holding leaders to the same protocol."
